### New talents in WoW Forever

- **Mutilate** Assassination _New_ Instantly attacks with both weapons for 75% weapon damage plus an additional 17 with each weapon. Damage increased by 20% against Poisoned targets. Awards 2 Combo Points.
- **Venom** Assassination _New_ Finishing move that increases the damage of your Poisons by 30% and your chance to apply Poisons by 10%. Lasts longer per combo point:
1 point : 9 sec
2 points: 12 sec
3 points: 15 sec
4 points: 18 sec
5 points: 21 sec
- **Puncturing Wounds** Combat _New_ Increases the critical strike chance of your Backstab by 10% and your Mutilate by 5%, and gives Backstab a 15% chance to add an additional Combo Point.
- **Flawless Execution** Combat _New_ Reduces the Energy cost of your Eviscerate ability by 10.
- **Dirty Tricks** Subtlety _New_ Reduces the Energy cost of your Sap and Blind abilities by 25%.
- **Improved Distract** Subtlety _New_ Increases the radius of your Distract ability by 3 yds, and further reduces the Stealth detection of distracted enemies as though they were an additional 1 level lower.
- **Quietus** Subtlety _New_ Your Sinister Strike, Ghostly Strike, and Hemorrhage abilities cause 2% more damage against targets below 35% health.
- **Cutthroat** Subtlety _New_ Your Backstab has a 3% chance to cause your next Ambush within 10 sec to not require Stealth.
- **Thousand Cuts** Subtlety _New_ When your Rupture ability deals periodic damage, the Energy cost of your next Hemorrhage or Backstab ability within 10 sec is reduced by 3, stacking up to 5 times.

- **Improved Expose Armor** Assassination _Changed_ Reduces the Energy cost of your Expose Armor ability by 5, and refunds 1 Combo Points when cast with 5 Combo Points.Classic: Increases the armor reduced by your Expose Armor ability by 25%.
- **Lethality** Assassination _Changed_ Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Sinister Strike, Gouge, Backstab, Mutilate, Ghostly Strike, and Hemorrhage abilities by 4%.Classic: Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Sinister Strike, Gouge, Backstab, Ghostly Strike, and Hemorrhage abilities by 6%.

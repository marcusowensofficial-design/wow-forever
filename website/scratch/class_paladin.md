### New talents in WoW Forever

- **Voice of Truth** Holy _New_ Grants you immunity to Silence and Interrupt effects for 6 sec.
- **Reverence** Holy _New_ Allows 10% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.
- **Purifying Power** Holy _New_ Reduces the mana cost of your Cleanse and Purify spells by 10% and reduces the cooldown of your Exorcism and Holy Wrath spells by 17%.
- **Infusion of Light** Holy _New_ Your Holy Shock and Flash of Light critical hits reduce the cast time of your next Holy Light cast within 15 sec by 0.5 sec.
- **Divine Precision** Holy _New_ Improves your chance to hit with Holy spells by 6%.
- **Consecrated Ground** Holy _New_ Gives your Holy spells 5% increased damage against the first 4 enemies that enter your Consecration.
- **Light's Vigil** Holy _New_ Applies Light's Vigil to the target for 30 sec. Your next Holy Shock cast on them triggers no cooldown and causes enemy targets to suffer 175 to 189 Holy damage and refund 75% of Light's Vigil's Mana cost, or allied targets to heal their party for 326 to 344. You may only have one Light's Vigil active per party.
- **Improved Seal of Fury** Protection _New_ When Seal of Fury's shield is fully absorbed, restore 0 Mana, increased by 15% per level the attacker is above you, up to 45%.
- **Sacred Duty** Protection _New_ Increases your total Stamina by 2% and reduces the cooldown of your Divine Shield, Divine Protection, and Templar's Bulwark spells by 30 sec.
- **Swift Judgement** Protection _New_ Finishes the remaining cooldown on your Judgement ability and reduces the Mana cost of your next Judgement by 100%.
- **Templar's Bulwark** Protection _New_ When activated, this ability grants you an absorb shield equal to 100% of your maximum health for 8 sec. Applies Forbearance for 1 min. Cannot be cast while Forbearance is active.
- **Iron Creed** Protection _New_ Increases the threat generated by your Holy Strike ability by 5%. While Righteous Fury is active, Holy Strike also reduces your damage taken by 2% for 6 sec.
- **Holy Conduit** Retribution _New_ Reduces the mana cost of your Consecration, Holy Wrath, Exorcism, and Hammer of Wrath spells by 20%.
- **Sanctified Judgement** Retribution _New_ Gives your Judgement ability a 33% chance to return 20% of the Mana cost of the judged seal.
- **Sacred Arbiter** Retribution _New_ Increases the damage of your Holy Strike ability by 20% and causes it to refresh all Judgement effects on the target.
- **Champion of the Light** Retribution _New_ Increases your spell damage by up to 20% of your Intellect.
- **Instrument of Law** Retribution _New_ Reduces the cast time of your Hammer of Wrath by 0.5 sec, and reduces all threat you generate by 10% while Righteous Fury is not active.
- **Twist of Light** Retribution _New_ Reduces the Mana cost of your Seal spells by 20%, and when you replace your Seal of Command, Seal of Righteousness, Seal of Fury, or Seal of Justice with a different Seal, you gain an Echo of that Seal. Your next melee attack applies the replaced Seal's effects, consuming the Echo.

### Talents changed from Classic

- **Healing Light** Holy _Changed_ Increases the amount healed by your Holy Light, Flash of Light, and Holy Shock spells by 4%.Classic: Increases the amount healed by your Holy Light and Flash of Light spells by 4%.
- **Spiritual Focus** Holy _Changed_ Gives your Flash of Light, Holy Light, and Light's Vigil spells a 35% chance to not lose casting time when you take damage.Classic: Gives your Flash of Light and Holy Light spells a 14% chance to not lose casting time when you take damage.
- **Improved Seals** Holy _Changed_ Increases the damage done by your Seals and Judgements by 5%.Classic: Increases the damage done by your Seal of Righteousness and Judgement of Righteousness by 3%.
- **Unyielding Faith** Holy _Changed_ Reduces the duration of Fear and Disorient effects on you by 15%.Classic: Increases your chance to resist Fear and Disorient effects by an additional 5%.
- **Illumination** Holy _Changed_ After getting a critical effect from your Flash of Light, Holy Light, Light's Vigil, or Holy Shock heal spell you have a 20% chance to gain Mana equal to 50% of the base cost of the spell.Classic: After getting a critical effect from your Flash of Light, Holy Light, or Holy Shock heal spell, gives you a 20% chance to gain Mana equal to the base cost of the spell.

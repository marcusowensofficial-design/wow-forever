### New talents in WoW Forever

- **Power in Light** Discipline _New_ Your Smite and Penance spells deal 2% increased damage to targets afflicted with your Holy Fire.
- **Twin Disciplines** Discipline _New_ Increases the damage and healing of your instant cast spells by 1%.
- **Holy Precision** Discipline _New_ Improves your chance to hit with Holy spells by 6%.
- **Soul Warding** Discipline _New_ Reduces the cooldown on your Power Word: Shield spell by 4 sec and reduces its mana cost by 15%.
- **Penance** Discipline _New_ Launches a volley of holy light at the target, causing 45 Holy damage to an enemy, or 134 healing to an ally, instantly and every 1 sec for 2 sec.
- **Renewed Hope** Discipline _New_ Your heals from Flash Heal, Binding Heal, Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal, and Penance gain 2% increased critical strike chance when cast on targets with Weakened Soul, and reduce the remaining duration of Weakened Soul on their target by 1 sec.
- **Divine Aegis** Discipline _New_ Your critical heals create a protective shield on the target, absorbing 5% of the amount healed. Lasts 12 sec.
- **Binding Heal** Holy _New_ Heals a friendly target and the caster for 236 to 284. Low threat.
- **Litany of Light** Holy _New_ When you cast a healing spell, gain Mana equal to 5% of the base cost of the spell if your previous heal was a different spell.
- **Prayer of Mending** Holy _New_ Places a spell on the target that heals them for 172 the next time they take damage or receive non-periodic healing. When the heal occurs, Prayer of Mending jumps to a party or raid member within 20 yards. Jumps up to 5 times and lasts 30 sec after each jump. This spell can only be placed on one target at a time per caster.
- **Improved Mind Flay** Shadow Magic _New_ Your Mind Flay now deals 10% more damage, gains 5 yards increased range, but slows the target's movement speed by 35%.
- **Devouring Contagion** Shadow Magic _New_ Reduces the mana cost of your Devouring Plague by 25%.

Targets that die while Devouring Plague is active spreads it, jumping to a nearby enemy within 5 yds for the remaining duration.
- **Early Demise** Shadow Magic _New_ Increases Shadow Word: Death's critical strike chance on targets at or below 20% health by 15%.

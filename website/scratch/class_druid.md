### New talents in WoW Forever

- **Genesis** Balance _New_ Increases the periodic damage and healing done by your spells and abilities by 1%.
- **Nature's Majesty** Balance _New_ Increases your critical strike chance with spells and melee attacks by 2%.
- **Nature's Splendor** Balance _New_ Increases the duration of your Moonfire and Rejuvenation spells by 3 sec, your Regrowth spell by 6 sec, and your Insect Swarm spell by 2 sec.
- **Eclipse** Balance _New_ Your Wrath spell reduces the cast time of your next 2 Starfire spells by 0.17 sec. Stores up to 4 charges. Lasts 15 sec.
- **Shifting Power** Feral Combat _New_ Instantly convert 55% of base Mana into 40 Energy. Shifting Power's cost is reduced by effects that reduce the cost of Shapeshifting.
- **Primal Bite** Feral Combat _New_ Bite the target, dealing 100% normal damage plus 26 and generating a high amount of threat.
- **Improved Shifting Power** Feral Combat _New_ Reduces the cooldown of your Shifting Power spell by 4 sec.
- **Natural Instinct** Feral Combat _New_ Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your melee abilities by 10% and increases your spell healing by 12% of your Intellect.
- **Natural Reaction** Feral Combat _New_ Increases your dodge chance by 1%, and gives you a 20% chance to gain 5 Rage each time you dodge.
- **Rend and Tear** Feral Combat _New_ Increases damage done by your melee abilities on Bleeding targets by 2%.
- **Berserk** Feral Combat _New_ Requires Cat Form, Bear Form, Dire Bear Form
Causes your Primal Bite ability to strike up to 3 targets, removes its cooldown, and increases the critical strike chance of your Combo Point-generating abilities by 100%. Clears and grants immunity to Fear effects for the duration. Lasts 15 sec.
- **Gift of the Earthmother** Restoration _New_ Reduces the global cooldown by 0.5 seconds on your Rejuvenation, Swiftmend, and Wild Growth spells.
- **Living Spirit** Restoration _New_ Increases your Spirit by 5%.
- **Wild Growth** Restoration _New_ Heals the target and their party for 336 over 7 sec. Party members must be within 43.5 yards of target. The amount healed is applied quickly at first, and slows down as Wild Growth reaches its full duration.

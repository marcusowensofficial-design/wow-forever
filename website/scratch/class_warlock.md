### New talents in WoW Forever

- **Malediction** Affliction _New_ Increases all periodic damage done by your Warlock spells by 1%.
- **Soul Harvest** Affliction _New_ Killing a non-trivial target afflicted by your Drain Soul increases your Mana regeneration by 50% for 10 sec and allows 50% of normal Mana regeneration to continue while casting.
- **Improved Drains** Affliction _New_ Increases health drained or damage done by your Drain Life, Drain Soul, and Wrack spells by 7%.
- **Pandemic** Affliction _New_ Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Corruption, Bane of Agony, Bane of Doom, Drain Soul, Drain Life, Siphon Life, and Wrack spells by 33%.
- **Malevolence** Affliction _New_ Increases the critical effect chance of your Shadow spells by 1%.
- **Wrack** Affliction _New_ Tears the target apart from within, inflicting 36 Shadow damage every 1 sec and increasing the damage they take from your other Shadow damage over time effects by 10% for 6 sec.

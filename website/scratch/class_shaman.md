### New talents in WoW Forever

- **Lightning Overload** Elemental Combat _New_ Gives your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells a 3% chance to cast a second, similar spell on the same target at no additional cost that causes half damage and no threat.
- **Earthbound** Elemental Combat _New_ Your Earthbind Totem Immobilizes nearby targets for 5 sec when cast.
- **Lava Burst** Elemental Combat _New_ You hurl molten lava at the target, dealing 150 to 192 Fire damage. If your Flame Shock is on the target, Lava Burst deals 20% increased damage.
- **Mental Dexterity** Enhancement _New_ Increases your Attack Power by an amount equal to 33% of your Intellect.
- **Shamanistic Focus** Enhancement _New_ Reduces the mana cost of your Shock and Lightning Shield spells by 45%.
- **Mental Quickness** Enhancement _New_ Increases your spell damage and healing by up to 15% of your Intellect.
- **Improved Stormstrike** Enhancement _New_ When you Stormstrike, you have a 50% chance to gain 50% mana regeneration while casting spells for 15 sec, and Stormstrike's cooldown has a 50% chance to reset each time you Dodge or Parry.
- **Maelstrom Weapon** Enhancement _New_ When you deal damage with a melee attack, you have a chance to reduce the cast time and Mana cost of your next Lightning Bolt spell by 4%. Stacks up to 5 times. Lasts 30 sec.
- **Rage of the Farseer** Enhancement _New_ Increases your attack speed by 30% for 25 sec.
- **Mindfulness** Restoration _New_ Allows 17% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.
- **Water Shield** Restoration _New_ The caster is surrounded by 3 globes of water. When a spell, melee, or ranged attack hits the caster or when one of the caster's healing spells gets a critical result, 2% of maximum mana is restored to the caster, expending one water globe. Only one globe will activate every few seconds. Lasts 10 min.

Only one Elemental Shield can be active on the Shaman at any one time.
- **Riptide** Restoration _New_ Heals a friendly target for 486 to 534, an additional 445 over 15 sec, and increases the effectiveness of your Chain Heal casts directly on that target by 25%.

- **Reverberation** Elemental Combat _Changed_ Reduces the cooldown of your Shock spells by 0.2 sec.
- **Call of Flame** Elemental Combat _Changed_ Increases the damage done by your Fire Totems and by your Flame Shock, Fire Nova, and Lava Burst spells by 5%.Classic: Increases the damage done by your Fire Totems by 5%.
- **Elemental Devastation** Elemental Combat _Changed_ Your offensive spell critical strikes will increase your chance to get a critical strike with melee attacks by 3% for 10 sec.Classic: Your offensive spell crits will increase your chance to get a critical strike with melee attacks by 3% for 10 sec.

🔥 Cooperative Open World Utility

The Campfire Station System

Any standard Basic Campfire can be evolved into an encampment. Party members can place crafted profession stations around the fire to provide on-site crafting and grant party-wide 1-hour buffs that mirror class blessings. Buffs do not stack with the class versions and provide approximately 80% of their power (e.g. 8% vs 10%).

🎒 Verified Core Camping Mechanics

  • ✦ Ironwood Campfire (Level 25+): 60-min campfire with 25-yd aura, supporting 2 stations at +15% enhanced buff potency.
  • ✦ Silk Encampment Tent: Tailoring 125 shelter accelerating Rested XP generation to 200% of standard inn velocity.
  • ✦ Cozy Sleeping Bag Synergy: Resting in a tent with a sleeping bag grants +5% bonus monster kill EXP for 2 hours.
  • ✦ Camp Cooking Feasts (+5% Kill XP): Well Fed meals provide primary stats plus +6 MP5 (Sagefish) and +5% bonus EXP.
  • ✦ Dedicated Reagent Bag Slot: Trade materials pull directly into on-site camp stations without inventory clutter.

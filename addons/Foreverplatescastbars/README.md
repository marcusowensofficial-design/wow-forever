# ⚔️ ForeverPlates Castbars (Forever Nameplates Castbars)

**Pixel-Perfect Nameplate & Unit Frame Castbar Engine with Latency, Target Indicator, Spell Shield, and OLED Theme for World of Warcraft: Forever (Camelot 12.0 Engine / TOC 16001).**

---

## 🌟 Overview
ForeverPlates Castbars is an official project of the **WoW Forever Addon Suite**, developed by Marcus Owens. Built to integrate seamlessly with `ForeverPlates` or standalone unit frames:
- **Enemy Nameplate Castbars**: Positions high-contrast, sub-pixel castbars directly below enemy nameplates.
- **Player, Target & Focus Castbars**: Full standalone castbar tracking with cast time remaining/total, spell name, and spell icon.
- **Latency Indicator**: Visualizes network latency lag tolerance window at the end of casts for optimal spell queueing.
- **Uninterruptible Spell Shield**: Dynamic silver/shield border highlighting non-kickable spell casts.
- **Target Indicator & OLED Dark Themes**: Clean visual contrast without frame clutter or FPS degradation.
- **Zero-Taint Architecture**: Completely isolated from protected unit frame tables under the 12.0 Camelot engine.

## 🛠️ Slash Commands
- `/bcb` or `/bettercastbars` or `/fpc`: Open the configuration and positioning GUI.

## 📁 Installation
Place into your WoW Forever AddOns directory:
```
World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns\Foreverplatescastbars\
```

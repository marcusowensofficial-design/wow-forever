🔥 Cooperative Open World Utility

The Campfire Station System

Any standard Basic Campfire can be evolved into an encampment. Party members can place crafted profession stations around the fire to provide on-site crafting and grant party-wide 1-hour buffs that mirror class blessings. Buffs do not stack with the class versions and provide approximately 80% of their power (e.g. 8% vs 10%).

🎒 Verified Phase 2 Beta Camp Mechanics

  • ✦ Ironwood Campfire (Level 25+): 60-min campfire with 25-yd aura, supporting 2 stations at +15% enhanced buff potency.
  • ✦ Silk Encampment Tent: Tailoring 125 shelter accelerating Rested XP generation to 200% of standard inn velocity.
  • ✦ Cozy Sleeping Bag Synergy: Resting in a tent with a sleeping bag grants +5% bonus monster kill EXP for 2 hours.
  • ✦ Camp Cooking Feasts (+5% Kill XP): Well Fed meals provide primary stats plus +6 MP5 (Sagefish) and +5% bonus EXP.

🤖 Architectural Briefing: How Native Cooldowns Affect AI, Automation & Addon Design

Why AIs & Bots Cannot Scrape Cooldowns: In the 12.0 Camelot engine, Blizzard's secret value architecture explicitly restricts combat Lua queries. Spell cooldown durations and buff timestamps return protected <secret number> tokens that cannot be read, compared, or computed by third-party scripts. The Cooldown Manager renders text via C++ internal routines, acting as a human visual aid while deliberately preventing AI combat rotation solvers.
Should We Build Custom Cooldown Addons? No — attempting to build a full combat WeakAura replacement in Lua will inevitably trigger execution taint cascades during raid encounters. The winning strategy is symbiosis: let Blizzard handle the secure combat cooldown dock, while our companion addons provide optional non-intrusive cosmetic skinning (1px borders, cyberpunk color palettes via hooksecurefunc) without touching secure logic.

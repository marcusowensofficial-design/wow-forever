✦ New Playable Race

The Skyborne

Descendants of high elves and elemental wind-weavers who took refuge on Zephras Isle during the Great Sundering. Masters of thermal updrafts and arcane currents, the Skyborne have descended back to Azeroth as a neutral race, forging alliances with both factions.

Faction Allegiance Choice (Determines 5th Class Option)

High Order Skyborne (Alliance) Alliance allegiance under Ironforge scholars. Unlocks exclusive Mage class (arcane & fire thermals).
Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) Horde allegiance guided by Tauren shamanism & Skywall pacts. Unlocks exclusive Shaman class (totems & lightning).
Universal Classes (Both Factions): Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid
Faction-Exclusive Classes: Mage (Alliance High Order) • Shaman (Horde Windshapers)
Starting Realm: Zephras Isle (Floating zone above western Kalimdor)

🔥 Cooperative Open World Utility

The Campfire Station System

Any standard Basic Campfire can be evolved into an encampment. Party members can place crafted profession stations around the fire to provide on-site crafting and grant party-wide 1-hour buffs that mirror class blessings. Buffs do not stack with the class versions and provide approximately 80% of their power (e.g. 8% vs 10%).

🎒 Verified In-Game Camp Mechanics

  • ✦ Camp Benefits Aura (60 min): Unified outdoor aura granting +8% All Stats (Fish Bowl), +Strength (Sharpening Wheel).
  • ✦ The Tent (Hourly Rested XP): Shelters players to grant field rested XP on a 1-hour internal cooldown.
  • ✦ Camp Cooking (+5% Kill XP): Well Fed meals (Herb Baked Egg) provide stats plus +5% bonus EXP from monster kills.
  • ✦ Dedicated Reagent Bag Slot: Equip an extra 6th bag purely for trade reagents with zero vendor reagents required.

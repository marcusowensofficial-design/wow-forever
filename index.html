✦ New Playable Race

The Skyborne

Descendants of high elves and elemental wind-weavers who took refuge on Zephras Isle during the Great Sundering. Masters of thermal updrafts and arcane currents, the Skyborne have descended back to Azeroth as a neutral race, forging alliances with both factions.

Faction Allegiance Choice (Determines 5th Class Option)

High Order Skyborne (Alliance) Alliance allegiance under Ironforge scholars. Unlocks exclusive Mage class (arcane & fire thermals).
Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) Horde allegiance guided by Tauren shamanism & Skywall pacts. Unlocks exclusive Shaman class (totems & lightning).
Universal Classes (Both Factions): Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid
Faction-Exclusive Classes: Mage (Alliance High Order) • Shaman (Horde Windshapers)
Starting Realm: Zephras Isle (Floating zone above western Kalimdor)

The Camping System

Cooperative Outdoor Buff Hubs
  • Turns any open-world campfire into a resting, cooking, and trading encampment.
  • Players can place up to 3 crafted profession objects around the fire (1 per party member).
  • Blacksmith's Anvil: +10% Attack Power for 1 hour.
  • Weaver's Loom: +Spirit & MP5 mana regeneration.
  • Alchemist's Still: +10% potion and elixir effectiveness.
  • Includes 600+ new trade blueprints dropping from dungeon bosses!

Realmless Ruleset Engine

In-Combat Addon Disarmament
